# Executive Summary — Illustrative Pricing & ROI Analysis

**Scope:** Pricing and unit-economics scenarios for a fictional product using documented assumptions; no real company or market data is represented.

## Headline results

- Base-case price / volume: **$110 / 10,000 units**.
- Base-case break-even: **3,871 units**.
- Base-case operating profit: **$380,000** and ROI of **-24.0%**.
- Highest modeled ROI: **Upside at 36.0%**.

## Decision readout

The base case clears break-even by **6,129 units**. The price-volume matrix shows that volume scale is a material lever, but price increases improve contribution margin only when demand is assumed to hold. Pricing decisions should therefore pair the margin view with demand evidence.

## Controls

- Break-even returns `None` when contribution per unit is non-positive.
- ROI returns `None` when investment is zero.
- Scenario outputs use the same revenue, contribution, break-even, and ROI calculation path.

## Limitations

This is an illustrative portfolio artifact built from synthetic assumptions. It does not estimate a demand curve, customer willingness to pay, taxes, financing, churn, retention, or competitive response.
